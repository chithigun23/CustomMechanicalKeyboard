3D PRINT FILES
==============

Print these three. Nothing needs support.

  keyboard_case.stl          361.8 x 139.8 x 20.5   needs a 400 mm-class bed
                                                    (388 mm on the diagonal)
  keyboard_plate.stl         357.2 x 135.2 x 3.6
  encoder_knob_D_shaft.stl   20.0 dia x 21.3

The .step files are the same parts if your slicer prefers them (Bambu Studio and
PrusaSlicer both take STEP and will give slightly cleaner curves than the STL).

  assembly_print.step   reference only. Open it to see how the parts sit together.

SETTINGS
--------
CASE     Floor down. 0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters, 25%+ infill.

PLATE    Face down, so the relief pockets end up on the top face and need no
         bridging. 0.4 mm nozzle - the thinnest web is 1.14 mm, next to the
         Caps Lock stabiliser. PETG or ABS is worth it over PLA.
         At 1.4 mm over a 354 mm span it will feel floppy in your hands before
         it is bolted down. That is expected and stops mattering once it is on
         the 18 collars and the perimeter ledge.
         DO NOT make the plate thicker. There is only 0.40 mm of clearance above
         it before a bottomed-out keycap lands on it.

KNOB     Flat end down, no support. The 24 flutes print cleanly.
         This is the D-bore version - no grub screw needed, it presses straight
         onto the flat on the encoder shaft.


SCREWS AND HARDWARE
===================
Two screw lengths cover every combination. M3 x 0.5 throughout.

WITH THE TOP PLATE  (grip = 1.4 plate + 2.2 collar + 1.6 PCB = 5.2 mm)
  18 x  M3 x 10  countersunk 90 deg, ISO 14581, TORX T10
        Machined case: 4.8 mm into the tapped hole.
        Printed case:  4.8 mm into the insert. M3 x 12 also fits here and gets
                       the full 5.7 mm of insert, but do NOT use 12 on the
                       machined case - it may bottom out past the tapped depth.

WITHOUT THE TOP PLATE  (grip = 1.6 mm PCB only)
  18 x  M3 x 6   button or pan head, ISO 14583 / 7380-TX, TORX T10
        4.4 mm engagement, safe on both cases.
        Printed case only: M3 x 8 works too and takes the full insert.
        Do not use a countersunk screw here - the PCB holes are not chamfered.
        Optional: nylon washer under each head to protect the solder mask.

Torx drive size for M3 in all of these is T10. A few makers use T8 on M3 flat
heads, so check the listing.
Finish: A2 stainless is the default; black oxide or black zinc looks better
against a black anodised case.

ALSO NEEDED
  6 x  dia 12-14 self-adhesive rubber foot (six dia 14 x 1.0 recesses underneath)

  18 x  M3 heat-set insert, OD 4.6 x 5.7 long
        (Ruthex RX-M3x5.7, CNC Kitchen M3 standard, or equivalent)
        The holes in the case are dia 4.0 x 6.2 deep, which is the recommended
        hole for that insert - heat-set inserts melt into a hole SMALLER than
        their own OD, so 4.0 is correct, it is not a typo.
        If you have short inserts (OD 4.0 x 4.0), they need a dia 3.5 hole -
        set INSERT_D = 3.5, INSERT_L = 4.5 in keyboard_cad.py and re-export.
