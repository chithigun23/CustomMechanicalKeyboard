CNC FILES - what to upload to JLC
=================================

Upload these three. Everything here is in mm.

  keyboard_case_cnc_tapped.step     361.8 x 139.8 x 20.5    ~534 g in 6061
  keyboard_plate.step               357.2 x 135.2 x 3.6     ~95 g in 6061
  encoder_knob_cnc_grubscrew.step   20.0 dia x 21.3         ~37 g in brass

  assembly_cnc.step   reference only - do NOT upload. Open it to see how the
                      three parts sit together.

SETTINGS
--------
CASE     Aluminium 6061-T6
         Bead blast + black anodise (Type II)
         ISO 2768-m
         Threads: M3 x 0.5, 18 places, 5 mm min full thread
         Break all sharp edges 0.2 mm

PLATE    Aluminium 6061-T6
         Bead blast + anodise (black to match, or clear)
         ISO 2768-m, EXCEPT the outline - see notes
         Deburr both faces - there are 89 through-cutouts

KNOB     Brass H62 (as-machined or polished), or 6061 anodised to match
         ISO 2768-m
         Threads: M3 x 0.5, 1 place (the grub screw hole)

ORDER NOTES - paste these into the comments box
-----------------------------------------------
  "18 x dia 2.5 holes to be tapped M3 x 0.5, 5 mm min full thread."
  "Threads to be free of anodise, or chased after anodising."
  "Case pocket 354.8 x 132.8 must not be undersize (+0.3 / -0). PCB seat."
  "Case boss top faces 9.00 +/-0.1 from the outer bottom face."
  "Plate outline 357.2 x 135.2 must not be oversize (-0.3 / +0)."
  "Internal corners where the boss ribs meet the wall may be radiused up to R2."

The two tightest items are the boss height (it sets the keycap clearance, which
is only 0.40 mm) and the plate outline (it drops into a 357.8 pocket, and the
default 2768-m tolerance at that size is too loose on its own).

DO NOT use powder coat - at 60-100 um it is thick enough to matter in the PCB
pocket. Anodising at 10-25 um is fine.


SCREWS AND HARDWARE
===================
Two screw lengths cover every combination. M3 x 0.5 throughout.

WITH THE TOP PLATE  (grip = 1.4 plate + 2.2 collar + 1.6 PCB = 5.2 mm)
  18 x  M3 x 10  countersunk 90 deg, ISO 14581, TORX T10
        Machined case: 4.8 mm into the tapped hole.
        Printed case:  4.8 mm into the insert. M3 x 12 also fits here and gets
                       the full 5.7 mm of insert, but do NOT use 12 on the
                       machined case - it may bottom out past the tapped depth.

WITHOUT THE TOP PLATE  (grip = 1.6 mm PCB only)
  18 x  M3 x 6   button or pan head, ISO 14583 / 7380-TX, TORX T10
        4.4 mm engagement, safe on both cases.
        Printed case only: M3 x 8 works too and takes the full insert.
        Do not use a countersunk screw here - the PCB holes are not chamfered.
        Optional: nylon washer under each head to protect the solder mask.

Torx drive size for M3 in all of these is T10. A few makers use T8 on M3 flat
heads, so check the listing.
Finish: A2 stainless is the default; black oxide or black zinc looks better
against a black anodised case.

ALSO NEEDED
  6 x  dia 12-14 self-adhesive rubber foot (six dia 14 x 1.0 recesses underneath)

   1 x  M3 x 10 cup-point set screw, for the knob
        Hex socket 1.5 mm A/F. The hole is tapped the full 6.9 mm of wall and
        the shaft flat is 8.5 mm in from the OD, so a 10 is the shortest that
        reaches it. It ends up about 1.5 mm proud of the knob - cosmetic only.


  No heat-set inserts on this route - the 18 bosses are tapped M3 directly.
