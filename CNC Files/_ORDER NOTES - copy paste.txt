===============================================================
IF THERE IS ROOM (order-level comment box)
===============================================================
These are worth having but nothing breaks without them:

  - Break all sharp edges 0.2 mm on the case top rim.
  - Plate: deburr both faces, 89 through-cutouts.
  - Plate: any workholding tab witness marks on the underside only.
  - No laser marking on visible faces.


===============================================================
WHAT GOT CUT AND WHY
===============================================================
Dropped as not worth the space:

  "threads free of anodise"   Type II is a few microns per flank; the screw
                              chases it on the way in.
  "do not powder coat"        the finish dropdown already says anodise.
  "do not polish flutes shut" nobody polishes a bead-blast part.

Every remaining line is a dimension that makes a part unusable if it goes the
wrong way. Nothing is explained on purpose - it is all machinist-readable as is.
